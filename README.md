The original project document outlines the standard baseline requirements. However, completing the baseline ahead of schedule does not mean the project is finished. You are expected to use any remaining time to expand the scope, innovate, and add high-value features that elevate the project from functional to exceptional.


A Subject Matter Expert (SME) will evaluate your final output. To prepare for the SME Quality Audit, you must adhere to the following rules:

Identify and Improvise: The moment you feel a feature can be enhanced or optimized, go ahead and build it. Do not limit your team to the minimum requirements.

Brainstorm Value-Add Features: If you complete the baseline early, think critically about what new, unrequested features will add significant value to the project. Every enhancement must adhere to the core project principles and support the ultimate project goal without breaking the existing architecture.

Document and Map Your Enhancements: Keep a precise record of all modifications. Note down exactly what was a "stated feature" (required by the document) versus what was an "improvised feature" (added by your team).

Prepare Your Presentation Defense: During your final presentation and SME review, you must explicitly highlight your value additions. 
Be ready to present:
Stated Features: What you built to satisfy the standard requirements.

Improvised Features: The extra fine-tuning and advanced features you introduced.

The Business/Technical Benefit: The clear value, efficiency, or advantage these new features bring to the project.
